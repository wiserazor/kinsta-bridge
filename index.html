How we review

Most "best of" lists are written to rank, not to help. We hold ourselves to four rules, and they apply to every category on this site:

Compare on merits Real pricing (including renewal rates), measured performance, and support quality — not marketing badges.
No single winner We match options to situations. The best choice for an agency is rarely the best choice for a first-time blogger.
Name the trade-offs Every option has a downside. If a host is expensive, slow to scale, or strict about plugins, we say so plainly.
